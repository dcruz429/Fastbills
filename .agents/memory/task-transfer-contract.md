---
name: Verification-to-billing continuity
description: Product expectations for insurance and deadlines when moving verification work to billing.
---

Sending verification work to billing must retain its insurance and current due date, including insurance or IE-date changes submitted together with the transfer outcome. Preserve the current deadline rather than starting a new deadline from the transfer date.

**Why:** The user reported that billing work lost insurance and due dates and approved preserving both, including same-save edits. A transfer changes the workflow stage, not the underlying insurance or deadline.

**How to apply:** Treat transfers as using the effective edited record, including explicit clears and dates recalculated from an edited IE date. Do not substitute old values when a field was cleared, or silently reset deadlines on a stage change.
