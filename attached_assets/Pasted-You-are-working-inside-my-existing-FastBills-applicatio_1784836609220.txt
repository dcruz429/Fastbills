PROJECT OBJECTIVE

Add a Call Center Calendar that lets staff and managers:

- See which patients must be called today.
- Schedule patient calls for future dates and times.
- Record every call attempt and its outcome.
- Reschedule follow-up calls without erasing previous attempts.
- Assign calls to staff members.
- View calls by day, week, month, and agenda.
- Filter calls by department, employee, clinic, insurance, priority, task status, and call outcome.
- Identify overdue calls.
- Track call productivity and resolution rates.
- Maintain a complete audit history.
- Open the related FastBills task or patient record from the scheduled call.
- Keep task status separate from call outcome.

TASK STATUS VERSUS CALL OUTCOME

This distinction is mandatory.

A FastBills task can remain Assigned or In Progress even when today’s call attempt is complete.

Examples:

- Task Status: Assigned
  Call Outcome: No Answer
  Call Status: Follow-Up Required

- Task Status: In Progress
  Call Outcome: Left Voicemail
  Call Status: Rescheduled

- Task Status: Completed
  Call Outcome: Resolved
  Call Status: Resolved

Do not automatically complete the FastBills task merely because a call attempt was recorded.

Only update the task status when:

- The user explicitly selects a task-status update, or
- A configurable business rule authorizes the update.

OVERDUE LOGIC

A call becomes overdue when:

- Its scheduled date and time have passed,
- It has not been resolved, completed, or cancelled,
- And no valid later follow-up has replaced it.

Overdue calls should:

- Appear with a red visual indicator.
- Be filterable.
- Appear on the user’s dashboard or My Tasks area when practical.
- Contribute to manager reporting.
- Create an in-app notification according to configured notification rules.
- Not generate repeated duplicate notifications every page refresh.

MISSED CALLS

At an appropriate scheduled job interval, calls that have passed their scheduled time without activity may be marked Missed or Overdue according to business rules.

Do not require the user to have the page open for overdue status to update.

Use the application’s existing scheduled-job mechanism. If no scheduled-job mechanism exists, implement the safest option supported by the current stack and Replit deployment.
